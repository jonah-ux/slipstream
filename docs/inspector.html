04 / honest scope

What this picture cannot claim

keep it real

The coordinates are explanatory fixtures. They do not come from a benchmark run, a hosted corpus, or a model.

  • Slipstream does not generate embeddings or judge semantic quality.
  • Benchmark numbers belong to slipstream bench on the selected local runtime.
  • Manifest verification proves index identity and runtime compatibility, not retrieval truth.
  • No remote service or sibling repo is required for the local core.

The fun part is seeing the path. The honest part is labeling the boundary.
